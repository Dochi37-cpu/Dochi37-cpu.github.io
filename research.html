E2P works at the interface between equipment physics, process systems, engineering data and technology deployment. Source technologies and application domains are translated through equipment reality, process and operation models, and engineering evidence into design, scale-up, operation and technology-selection decisions.

ACADEMIC POSITIONING

Owning the interface between process systems engineering and equipment reality.

E2P의 장기적 연구영역은 PSE와 high-fidelity equipment engineering 중간의 interface입니다. 어느 한 분야를 대체하는 것이 아니라 서로 다른 fidelity의 evidence가 plant-level feasible region과 engineering decision을 어떻게 바꾸는지 연구합니다.E2P's long-term research space is the interface between process systems engineering and high-fidelity equipment engineering. The goal is not to replace either field, but to determine how evidence across fidelities changes plant-level feasible regions and engineering decisions.

TRADITIONAL PSE

Process → Equipment

Flowsheet, synthesis, optimization, control and scheduling problems that introduce equipment constraints as required.

Primary novelty: process synthesis · optimization · control · scheduling
E2P SYSTEMS

Equipment Reality → Process Feasibility → Engineering Decision

Lift equipment transport, performance, degradation and operating limits into process-level representations, validate them against engineering evidence, and propagate the consequences into plant decisions.

Primary novelty: plant fidelity · operability · model credibility · decision relevance
HIGH-FIDELITY EQUIPMENT

Physics → Equipment

CFD/FEM and multiphysics models resolve detailed equipment or reactor behavior, spatial gradients and scale transitions.

Primary novelty: high-fidelity physics · spatial resolution · scale transition
Research white space. E2P는 고해상도 장치해석이나 공정모사를 대신 수행하는 service group이 아니라, 각 분야의 결과가 실제 설비·공정에서 어떤 운전·설계 결정을 허용하는지를 규명하는 interface owner를 지향합니다.E2P is not a service group that simply provides high-fidelity equipment simulations or process simulations. Its long-term role is to own the interface that determines which operating and design decisions are actually supported by combined evidence.
FOUR FLAGSHIP QUESTIONS

We own questions, not software territories.

연구 단위는 Aspen, COMSOL, CFD, AI 같은 도구가 아니라 실제 engineering decision을 바꾸는 질문입니다.The unit of research is not Aspen, COMSOL, CFD or AI; it is a question whose answer can change an engineering decision.

Cross-scale fidelity

어떤 equipment-level physics가 plant-level KPI와 설계 경계를 실제로 바꾸는가? 필요한 fidelity와 불필요한 fidelity를 구분합니다.

Which equipment-level physics actually changes plant-level KPIs and design boundaries? The objective is to distinguish necessary from unnecessary fidelity.

METHODOLOGY

From high-fidelity evidence to plant-level decisions.

모델은 하나의 해상도로 고정되지 않습니다. 연구질문과 decision sensitivity에 따라 high-fidelity model, reduced/gray-box model, process model과 dynamic model을 계층적으로 연결합니다.Models are not fixed at one fidelity. High-fidelity, reduced/gray-box, process and dynamic models are connected hierarchically according to the research question and decision sensitivity.

01 · PHYSICS

High-fidelity model

Transport · multiphysics · reactor/equipment behavior · spatial resolution

02 · REDUCTION

Reduced / gray-box model

Preserve the physics that matters while enabling system-level computation.

03 · SYSTEM

Process model

Mass/energy balances · recycle · utilities · equipment constraints · plant KPIs

04 · OPERATION

Dynamic feasibility

Part-load · transient behavior · degradation · feasible operating region

05 · DECISION

Engineering decision layer

Design basis · specification · scale-up · operating policy · deployment

EQUIPMENT-AWARE MODELING

Carry equipment reality upward

성능맵, 압력강하, 열·물질전달, 열화와 운전제약 중 plant-level 결과를 바꾸는 요소를 공정 모델에 보존합니다.

Preserve the equipment behavior—performance maps, pressure drop, transport, degradation and operating limits—that materially changes plant-level outcomes.

Representative tools: COMSOL · CFD/FEM · Python · reduced-order modeling
PROCESS & DYNAMICS

Translate physics into operability

steady-state flowsheet뿐 아니라 부분부하와 transient condition까지 고려해 실제 feasible operating envelope를 정의합니다.

Move beyond steady-state flowsheets to define feasible operating envelopes under part-load and transient conditions.

Representative tools: Aspen Plus · Aspen HYSYS · dynamic simulation · optimization
ASSESSMENT & DECISION

Ask whether conclusions change

LCA, TEA, CAPEX/OPEX, GHG와 uncertainty를 이용해 더 높은 model fidelity가 실제 기술선택과 scale-up 결론을 바꾸는지 확인합니다.

Use LCA, TEA, CAPEX/OPEX, GHG and uncertainty to test whether higher model fidelity changes technology-selection and scale-up conclusions.

Methods: TEA · LCA · multi-objective optimization · uncertainty / sensitivity analysis
EVIDENCE-FIRST MODELING

Simulation is not the source of truth.

실제 설계자료와 실증 데이터가 simulation보다 우선합니다. 모델 업데이트의 목적은 단순한 fit 향상이 아니라 engineering decision의 신뢰도를 높이는 것입니다.Engineering documents and demonstration data take precedence over simulation. Model updates are performed to improve decision credibility, not simply to obtain a better fit.
