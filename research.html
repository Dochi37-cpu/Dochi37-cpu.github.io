장치 현실을
공정 feasible region과 의사결정으로Turning equipment reality into
process feasibility and decisions.

E2P는 traditional PSE와 high-fidelity equipment engineering 사이의 interface를 연구합니다. 핵심 질문은 “어떤 장치 물리가 plant-level 결과를 바꾸고, 그 근거를 어디까지 신뢰하며, 실제로 어떤 결정을 달리해야 하는가?”입니다.

E2P studies the interface between traditional process systems engineering and high-fidelity equipment engineering. The central question is which equipment physics changes plant-level outcomes, how far the evidence can be trusted and which decisions should therefore change.

ACADEMIC POSITIONING

Owning the interface between PSE and equipment reality.

어느 한 분야를 대체하는 것이 아니라 서로 다른 fidelity의 evidence가 plant-level feasible region과 engineering decision을 어떻게 바꾸는지 규명합니다.The aim is not to replace either field, but to determine how evidence across fidelities changes plant-level feasible regions and engineering decisions.

TRADITIONAL PSE

Process → Equipment

Flowsheets, synthesis, optimization, control and scheduling with equipment constraints introduced as required.

Primary novelty: synthesis · optimization · control · scheduling
E2P SYSTEMS

Equipment Reality → Process Feasibility → Engineering Decision

Lift equipment behavior into process-level representations, validate it against engineering evidence and propagate the consequences into plant decisions.

Primary novelty: plant fidelity · operability · model credibility · decision relevance
HIGH-FIDELITY EQUIPMENT

Physics → Equipment

CFD/FEM and multiphysics models resolve transport, reaction, spatial gradients and scale-transition behavior.

Primary novelty: high-fidelity physics · spatial resolution · scale transition
Boundary rule. E2P is not a simulation service group. A contribution should remain as a reusable model/data interface, a validated constraint or a decision-relevant research question.
FOUR FLAGSHIP QUESTIONS

We own questions, not software territories.

Aspen, COMSOL, CFD, Python 또는 AI는 질문을 검증하기 위한 도구입니다. 연구의 단위는 실제 engineering decision을 바꾸는 question입니다.Aspen, COMSOL, CFD, Python and AI are tools used to test a question. The unit of research is a question that can change an engineering decision.

MODEL HIERARCHY

From physics to the decision layer.

모델은 한 해상도로 고정하지 않습니다. decision sensitivity에 따라 필요한 물리를 보존하면서 high-fidelity, reduced/gray-box, process와 dynamic model을 계층적으로 연결합니다.Models are not fixed at one resolution. High-fidelity, reduced/gray-box, process and dynamic models are connected according to decision sensitivity while preserving the physics that matters.

Engineering decision layer

EVIDENCE-FIRST MODELING

Simulation is not the source of truth.

모델의 목적은 단순히 결과를 맞추는 것이 아니라 어떤 조건에서 믿을 수 있고 어떤 결정을 허용하는지를 명확히 하는 것입니다.The objective is not merely to improve fit, but to establish when a model can be trusted and which decisions the evidence supports.
