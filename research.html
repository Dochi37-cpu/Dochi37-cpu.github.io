RESEARCH · PLANT BOP & ENERGY SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

원천기술의 성능을 실제 플랜트의
설계·운전·scale-up 판단으로 연결합니다.Translate core-technology performance into
plant design, operation and scale-up decisions.

P2E는 촉매·반응기·분리기 자체를 모두 개발하는 연구그룹이 아니라, 검증된 원천기술이 실제 플랜트가 될 때 필요한 BOP, 유틸리티, 공정통합과 운전제약을 연구합니다.

P2E does not seek to develop every catalyst, reactor or separation technology itself. We study the BOP, utilities, plant integration and operating constraints required to translate validated core technologies into operable plant systems.

ACADEMIC POSITIONING

Owning the plant-system layer between core technology and deployment.

P2E는 검증된 core-technology 성능을 출발점으로 BOP·유틸리티·plant integration·operability와 그로부터 도출되는 scale-up decision을 연구합니다.P2E starts from validated core-technology performance and focuses on BOP, utilities, plant integration, operability and the resulting scale-up decisions.

SOURCE TECHNOLOGY

Reaction / Separation / Device → Performance

Experiments, kinetics, reactor or separation models, CFD/multiphysics results and source-technology specifications enter as validated upstream inputs.

Typical ownership: source-technology researchers and collaborators
P2E SYSTEMS

실제 플랜트에서는 무엇이 가능한가?What is feasible in the actual plant?

BOP & Utilities → Plant Integration → Operability

Integrate real BOP performance, engineering data and operating constraints with core process technologies to define feasible plant systems.

Primary novelty: plant feasibility · operability · scale-up · decision relevance
DECISION

그래서 무엇을 설계하고 검증해야 하는가?What should be designed or validated next?

Optimization / AI / TEA / LCA → Deployment

Optimization, AI, autonomous-operation methods, TEA and LCA are enabling or collaborative interfaces when they support plant-system decisions.

Used as needed; not the top-level P2E identity
Translation rule. Validated reactor/device models enter as Core Technology Input; P2E builds on them with BOP, plant integration and operability evidence. The endpoint is a decision, not a model.
FOUR FLAGSHIP QUESTIONS

We own questions, not software territories.

Aspen, COMSOL, CFD, Python, AI, TEA와 LCA는 질문을 검증하기 위한 수단입니다. 연구의 단위는 실제 plant-system decision을 바꾸는 question입니다.Aspen, COMSOL, CFD, Python, AI, TEA and LCA are tools used to test a question. The unit of research is a question that can change a plant-system decision.

RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

핵심기술에서 실제 의사결정까지 무엇을 차례로 연결하는지 보여줍니다.The architecture shows what must be connected between a core technology and an engineering decision.

연구 architecture는 upstream 원천기술을 모두 직접 개발하는 구조가 아니라, 검증된 Core Technology Input을 받아 BOP와 plant-system evidence를 축적하는 translation chain입니다.The architecture does not require P2E to develop every upstream technology. It accepts validated Core Technology Input and builds reusable BOP and plant-system evidence through a translation chain.

EVIDENCE-FIRST MODELING

Models do not define plant reality; engineering evidence does.

모델의 목적은 결과를 그럴듯하게 맞추는 것이 아니라 어떤 조건에서 믿을 수 있고 어떤 engineering decision을 허용하는지 명확히 하는 것입니다.The objective is not merely to improve fit, but to establish when a model can be trusted and which engineering decisions the evidence supports.
