RESEARCH CONTINUITY

From “Is the technology promising?” to “Under what conditions should it be adopted?”

박사과정에서는 저 TRL CO₂ 전환기술의 성능평가, TRL, LCA/TEA와 상용화 판단을 다뤘고, 산업체에서는 실제 plant engineering을 통해 장치, BOP, 설계기준, CAPEX/OPEX와 scale-up을 경험했습니다. 2025년 8월 15일 KRICT 합류 이후 이 두 문제의식을 실제 설계자료와 실증 데이터를 사용하는 process model, operability와 engineering decision으로 통합하고 있습니다.

Doctoral work addressed performance evaluation, TRL, LCA/TEA and commercialization decisions for low-TRL CO₂ conversion. Industrial plant-engineering experience added equipment, BOP, design basis, CAPEX/OPEX and scale-up. Since joining KRICT on 15 August 2025, these perspectives have been integrated into evidence-based process models, operability and engineering decisions.

Research identityEquipment-aware PSE · technology translation · engineering decisions
Current testbedse-SAF · CO₂-to-methanol · sustainable chemicals · H₂/electrolysis
Evidence basePFD/HMB · datasheets · performance maps · pilot/demonstration data

At E2P, technologies are testbeds. The enduring asset is a methodology for translating equipment reality into process-system understanding and defensible decisions.
