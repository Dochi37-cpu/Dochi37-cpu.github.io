IP PRINCIPLE

Protect what should remain reusable.

모든 연구결과를 patent로 만드는 것이 아니라 후속 기술과 scale-up에 재사용 가능한 핵심 interface와 implementation logic을 선별합니다.Not every result should become a patent. Protection is considered for reusable interfaces and implementation logic that matter to follow-on technology and scale-up.

DISCLOSE

Identify the invention

Record inventorship, technical contribution and evidence before public disclosure.

PROTECT

Choose the right mechanism

Patent, know-how, code/data governance or publication depending on strategic value.

TRANSFER

Connect to deployment

Link the protected asset to design, scale-up, licensing or operational implementation.
