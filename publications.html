Representative research trajectory

Thermochemical heat storage

Calcium-chloride-impregnated perlite and thermal-energy storage research established an early link between material behavior and system performance.

CO₂ photoconversion & assessment

Mesoporous photocatalysts, reactor-level photoconversion and life-cycle evaluation connected low-TRL source technologies to system-level feasibility questions.

Thermal & energy systems

Absorption heat pumps, thermal storage and spectral fluid filters expanded the work toward equipment/system design and engineering performance.

Plant-data-based process assessment

Industrial and demonstration engineering experience informs current work in validated process models, operability and decision-relevant analysis.

Electrolysis catalysts

Recent collaborative work includes durable electrodes and catalyst systems for electrolysis and saline operating conditions.
