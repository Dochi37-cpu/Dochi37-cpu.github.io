WHY THIS DISTINCTION MATTERS

Traceability is part of model credibility.

그룹이 새로 형성된 시점과 실제 연구가 수행된 맥락을 공개적으로 구분해야 P2E의 연구성과와 PI의 기존 기반을 모두 정확히 평가할 수 있습니다. “성과가 없다”는 의미가 아니라 현재 연구가 model reconstruction, validation과 manuscript development 단계에 있음을 정확히 표시하는 것입니다.

Publicly distinguishing when the group formed and where the work was performed allows both P2E outputs and the PI’s prior foundation to be evaluated accurately. This is not a claim of inactivity; it states that current work is in model reconstruction, validation and manuscript development.

An item will be labeled “P2E-originated” only when its research question, analysis and evidence were substantially developed through the P2E program.

AUTHORSHIP PRINCIPLE

Publication credit should reflect research ownership.

직접 지도 연구자가 자기 research question, analysis와 evidence를 소유하고 주저자로 설명할 수 있도록 합니다.Directly mentored researchers should own their research question, analysis and evidence and be able to represent the work as lead authors.

QUESTION

Own the framing

Understand why the problem matters and what decision the answer should change.

EVIDENCE

Own the analysis

Reproduce, validate, challenge assumptions and document traceability.

CREDIT

Represent the work

Authorship and professional visibility should follow substantive intellectual and technical ownership.
