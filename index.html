E2P Systems Group은 원천기술과 실제 장치의 물리·성능·운전 제약을 신뢰 가능한 공정모델과 operating envelope로 번역하고, 설계·실증 데이터를 통해 검증하여 scale-up, 설계, 운전 및 deployment 의사결정으로 연결하는 방법론을 연구합니다.

E2P Systems Group develops methods for translating source technologies and equipment reality into credible process models and operating envelopes, validating them with engineering and demonstration evidence, and using the resulting knowledge for design, scale-up, operation and deployment decisions.

WHAT DEFINES E2P

기술은 testbed이고,
연구의 본체는 방법론입니다.Technologies are testbeds.
The methodology is the research.

e-SAF, e-CCU, CO₂-to-methanol, FDCA, hydrogen/electrolysis는 서로 다른 응용 무대입니다. E2P가 축적하는 것은 장치 현실을 credible process representation으로 끌어올리고 실제 engineering decision으로 연결하는 방법론입니다.

e-SAF, e-CCU, CO₂-to-methanol, FDCA and hydrogen/electrolysis are different application environments. What E2P accumulates is a reusable methodology that lifts equipment reality into credible process representations and decisions.

01 · TRANSLATE

Technology Translation

장치의 physics, performance map, degradation, pressure drop와 operating constraint를 process-level model과 feasible region으로 번역합니다.

Translate equipment physics, performance maps, degradation, pressure drop and operating constraints into process-level models and feasible regions.

02 · VALIDATE

Evidence & Model Credibility

PFD/HMB, datasheet, pilot/plant data와 independent calculation을 이용해 validity domain과 uncertainty를 정의합니다.

Use PFD/HMB, datasheets, pilot/plant data and independent calculations to define validity domains and uncertainty.

03 · DECIDE

Engineering Decision-Making

모델 자체가 아니라 design basis, equipment specification, operating envelope, scale-up과 deployment 결정을 연구 결과로 만듭니다.

The research output is not the model itself, but defensible design bases, specifications, operating envelopes, scale-up and deployment decisions.

RESEARCH POSITIONING

Owning the interface between process systems engineering and equipment reality.

E2P의 academic white space는 서로 다른 fidelity의 지식과 데이터를 plant-level feasible region과 engineering decision으로 연결하는 interface입니다.E2P's academic white space is the interface that translates evidence across fidelities into plant-level feasible regions and engineering decisions.

TRADITIONAL PSE

Process → Equipment

Flowsheets, synthesis, optimization, control and scheduling with equipment constraints introduced as needed.

Primary novelty: synthesis · optimization · control · scheduling
E2P SYSTEMS

Equipment Reality → Process Feasibility → Engineering Decision

Lift equipment behavior into process-level representations, validate them with engineering evidence, and propagate the consequences into plant decisions.

Primary novelty: plant fidelity · operability · model credibility · decision relevance
HIGH-FIDELITY EQUIPMENT

Physics → Equipment

CFD/FEM, multiphysics and spatially resolved models explain detailed transport, reaction and scale-transition behavior.

Primary novelty: high-fidelity physics · spatial resolution · model hierarchy
